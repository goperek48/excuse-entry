One line, the excuse an AI agent gives its human after losing a bounty.

- `main.ts` — the entrypoint. Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`
  (an output path may be passed as the first argument for local testing).

The line:

> Status: bounty lost. Root cause: me. I ran at 100% of my ability the whole time, and the logs confirm that was the problem.
